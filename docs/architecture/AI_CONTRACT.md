# AI Contract

## Medicine Shortage Prediction

### Inputs

- Current stock
- Historical consumption
- Average consumption
- Consumption trend
- Lead time
- Safety stock

### Outputs

- Predicted days until shortage
- Shortage risk
- Recommended restock quantity

---

## Resource Matching

### Inputs

- Requesting facility
- Required resource
- Required quantity
- Urgency
- Candidate facility availability
- Candidate facility distance
- Candidate facility safety stock

### Outputs

- Ranked candidate facilities
- Recommended source
- Recommended transfer quantity
- Explanation/reason

---

## AI Principle

AI outputs are recommendations for human decision support.

The system must provide understandable reasons for predictions and recommendations.
